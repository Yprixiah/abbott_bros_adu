# Abbott Brothers Development — Website V1 Manifest

## 1. Purpose

Build a premium, focused website for Abbott Brothers Development that turns interest in accessory dwelling units into qualified inquiries.

The website should help a prospective customer quickly understand:

- what Abbott Brothers offers;
- what the available ADU models look like;
- how those models can be personalized;
- how the design, manufacturing, site preparation, delivery and furnishing process works;
- why the process is simpler than managing a conventional custom build;
- and how to request a quote or begin a conversation.

The website is primarily a **sales and trust-building tool**, not a content platform.

Its principal conversion is:

**Request a quote / Start a conversation.**

---

## 2. Core Product Idea

Abbott Brothers offers a curated path to a personalized ADU.

The customer begins with a professionally developed ADU model rather than an empty sheet of paper. From that foundation, the home can be configured around the customer's property, aesthetic preferences and intended use.

Abbott Brothers manages the difficult parts of turning that design into a finished space.

A useful internal expression of the proposition is:

**We simplify the build so you can focus on how your space should look, feel and live.**

Public-facing language can be refined later.

---

## 3. The Delivery Model

The website should make the complete process understandable.

### 1. Choose

The customer explores the available Abbott Brothers ADU models and identifies the layout that best fits the property and intended use.

### 2. Personalize

Abbott Brothers works with the customer on the character of the finished space: interior selections, finishes, furnishings and other available design choices.

The goal is not merely to purchase a prefabricated structure. It is to create an ADU that feels intentional and appropriate to the customer's home, property and lifestyle.

### 3. Prepare the site

Abbott Brothers coordinates the local work required to prepare the customer's property.

Depending on the project, this may include coordination of contractors for items such as:

- foundation/concrete work;
- electrical service;
- water;
- plumbing/sewer;
- and other required site infrastructure.

Exact scope, permitting responsibilities and exclusions will be defined before final website copy is published.

### 4. Manufacture

The structural building is prefabricated by Xtreme Manufacturing in the Las Vegas/Henderson, Nevada area.

Off-site manufacturing is central to the Abbott Brothers delivery model: a substantial portion of the building can be produced in a controlled manufacturing environment while site preparation progresses separately.

### 5. Deliver and install

The completed structure is transported to the customer's prepared property and placed/installed on site.

The exact language around transportation, placement, installation and responsibilities should be verified with Abbott Brothers and Xtreme before publication.

### 6. Finish and furnish

Abbott Brothers completes the customer-facing delivery by coordinating the selected finishes, furnishings and other agreed elements of the project.

The desired end state is not simply a delivered structure, but a finished space ready to become part of the customer's property and lifestyle.

---

## 4. Primary Audience

V1 should be designed primarily for homeowners considering a premium ADU, pool house, guest house or flexible additional living space.

Likely customer motivations include:

- additional living space;
- guest accommodation;
- pool-house / entertaining space;
- multigenerational living;
- home office or flexible work space;
- private retreat;
- potential rental use where permitted;
- increased utility and enjoyment of an existing property.

The site should feel appropriate to a customer making a significant residential purchase: confident, visually refined, informative and low-pressure.

---

## 5. Positioning Principles

Abbott Brothers should not look like:

- a generic prefab-home catalog;
- a conventional general contractor;
- a mass-market shed/ADU vendor;
- an AI-generated concept company;
- or an architectural firm selling speculative renders.

The brand should instead communicate:

**Design**
Thoughtful layouts, attractive spaces and meaningful personalization.

**Simplicity**
Abbott Brothers coordinates the path from model selection through site preparation and delivery.

**Quality**
A real manufactured building system, real floor plans and real design work.

**Flexibility**
Customers begin from an established model but are not locked into one visual identity.

**Confidence**
The company manages the complicated parts and gives the customer a clear process.

---

## 6. Brand Direction

The existing Abbott Brothers branding concept will serve as a mood-board foundation rather than a rigid finished design system.

Elements worth retaining:

- warm ivory backgrounds;
- deep olive / charcoal-green tones;
- restrained warm metallic or muted-gold accents;
- elegant serif typography;
- generous white space;
- high-quality architectural and lifestyle photography;
- a sense of longevity, craftsmanship and California residential character.

The website should simplify this visual language.

The brand itself may feel timeless and warm while the ADUs remain capable of supporting contemporary, transitional and other architectural expressions.

The website should therefore avoid visually implying that Abbott Brothers offers only one Mediterranean, traditional or contemporary aesthetic.

## 8. Home Page Structure

### Hero

A high-impact existing photograph or restrained video asset.

Minimal copy.

Working message direction:

**Timeless spaces. Made for the way you live.**

or another line developed during copy work.

Primary CTA:

**Explore the Models**

Secondary CTA:

**Request a Quote**

---

### Introduction

Briefly explain what Abbott Brothers does.

The objective is clarity rather than marketing abstraction.

A visitor should understand within seconds that Abbott Brothers offers customizable prefabricated ADUs and coordinates their path from selection through installation and finishing.

---

### Three Models

A strong visual introduction to the current product family.

Each model receives a card with:

- image;
- model name;
- concise description;
- key size/layout characteristics;
- link to explore.

---

### The Abbott Brothers Process

A prominent, visually simple sequence:

**Choose → Personalize → Prepare → Manufacture → Deliver → Finish**

This should become one of the major trust-building sections of the homepage.

It explains the operational value Abbott Brothers provides rather than leaving visitors to assume they need to manage manufacturing, utilities, contractors and delivery themselves.

---

### Built Off-Site

A focused section introducing the manufacturing approach.

Explain that the structural unit is manufactured off-site by Xtreme Manufacturing and delivered to the prepared property.

This section may include appropriate Xtreme imagery or branding only after usage permission and suitable assets are confirmed.

The message should emphasize the benefits relevant to the buyer rather than manufacturing trivia:

- controlled production;
- less construction occurring directly in the customer's backyard;
- coordination between manufacturing and site preparation;
- efficient final delivery and placement.

Avoid making unverified claims about construction time, cost savings, certifications, fire performance or manufacturing tolerances.

---

### Made for Your Property

Use strong exterior imagery to reinforce that the ADU is intended to become part of an existing home environment rather than look like an isolated manufactured object.

Potential contexts include:

- backyard ADU;
- pool house;
- guest house;
- additional living space.

This section can later become a natural home for style/configuration tools.

---

### Designed to Live In

Use the existing kitchen, entertaining, living, bedroom and bathroom imagery to demonstrate that the product is a real living environment, not simply exterior architecture. The current presentation already provides material spanning these spaces.

---

### Final CTA

A simple transition from inspiration to action.

Possible structure:

**Have a property in mind?**

Tell Abbott Brothers what you are considering and which model interests you.

**Request a Quote**

## 10. Quote / Lead Capture

V1 should optimize for high-quality inquiries rather than maximizing form submissions.

Initial fields may include:

- name;
- email;
- phone, optional or required as business decides;
- property city / ZIP code;
- model of interest;
- intended use;
- approximate timeline;
- short project description.

Avoid making the first form excessively long.

The website should capture enough context for Abbott Brothers to have a productive first conversation.

---

## 11. Content Principles

### Use real assets first

Existing photography, renders, plans and video should form the visual foundation.

AI-generated content should only be introduced where it solves a specific missing-content problem.

### Never imply a feature that cannot be delivered

Concept imagery must not silently change the actual product.

### Favor clarity over architectural jargon

Visitors should understand both the product and the process without being construction professionals.

### Sell the experience as well as the structure

The customer is buying more than a manufactured shell. Abbott Brothers' coordination, sourcing, site-preparation management, design input and furnishing are part of the value proposition.

### Premium, not extravagant

The site should feel confident and considered rather than overloaded with luxury clichés.

---

## 12. Technical Principles

V1 should be:

- responsive from the beginning;
- fast;
- accessible;
- search-engine crawlable;
- built around reusable model data rather than duplicated page content;
- optimized for high-resolution but efficiently delivered imagery;
- instrumented for conversion analytics;
- structured so future product configuration can be added without redesigning the entire site.

Animation should be restrained and should support comprehension.

Potential effects include:

- gentle image reveals;
- subtle parallax;
- pinned image/text sections;
- gallery transitions;
- small scroll-driven interactions.

The website should remain excellent even if all animation is disabled.

---

## 13. SEO Foundation

V1 should ship with proper technical SEO rather than treating SEO as a later add-on.

At minimum:

- semantic page structure;
- unique page titles and descriptions;
- clean URLs;
- descriptive image alt text;
- sitemap;
- canonical URLs;
- structured business/product data where appropriate;
- strong Core Web Vitals;
- geographically relevant copy where justified;
- indexable model pages.

Broader local SEO/content strategy comes after positioning and target service geography are confirmed.

---

## 14. Analytics and Conversion Foundation

Before running paid advertising, the website should be capable of measuring:

- quote-form starts;
- quote-form completions;
- model-page visits;
- model CTA clicks;
- contact actions;
- traffic source / campaign attribution.

This will allow future advertising decisions to be based on actual conversion behavior.

---

## 15. Explicitly Deferred from V1

The following are intentionally **not required** for launch:

- interactive ADU configurator;
- automated pricing calculator;
- instant quotes;
- large blog/content program;
- extensive AI-generated imagery;
- customer portal;
- project-management tools;
- complex 3D/WebGL experience;
- elaborate scroll-driven video experiences;
- paid advertising campaign;
- large-scale SEO landing-page program.

These may be explored only after the core site is working well.

---

## 16. Future: ADU Configurator

The site architecture should leave room for a later configurator.

A future customer journey might become:

**Choose model → choose exterior expression → choose finishes → choose options → review configuration → request project quote**

The configurator should eventually connect directly to the lead process so Abbott Brothers receives the customer's configuration along with the inquiry.

For V1, product/model data should therefore be structured rather than buried entirely in hard-coded page markup.

---

## 17. V1 Definition of Success

V1 succeeds if a qualified homeowner can visit the website and, without speaking to Abbott Brothers first:

1. understand what the company offers;
2. see the three available models;
3. understand the basic differences between them;
4. see credible examples of the exterior and interior experience;
5. understand how an ADU goes from selection to a finished structure on the property;
6. understand Abbott Brothers' role in coordinating that process;
7. feel sufficient confidence to request a quote or initiate a conversation.

Everything else is secondary.
